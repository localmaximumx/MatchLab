The Shot Conversion Law

Empirical correlation between in-match metrics and goal production.

Shots on Target to Goal Ratio: ~31.8%

Approximately 1 in every 3.15 shots on target results in a goal. This is the single strongest predictor of offensive output ($r = 0.584$).

Total Shots to Goal Ratio: ~11.2%

Total shots correlate moderately ($r = 0.381$), but contain noise from low-probability long-range attempts.

Corner Kick Conversion: ~2.8%

Only 2.8% of corners lead directly to a goal, proving that corner count is a secondary indicator of territory rather than direct conversion.
